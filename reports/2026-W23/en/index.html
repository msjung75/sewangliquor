■ Finding ① The "Sober" Generation — Drinking Culture Itself Has Changed

Among those in their 20s–30s, "drinking lightly" rather than "until drunk" has become the norm (OhmyNews 2026-05-02,

SportsQ 2026-05-27). Healthy pleasure—enjoying health and self-care—has entered drinking occasions, cementing an

attitude that keeps alcohol's social function while shedding its downsides. On Instagram, #SoberCurious · #lightweight-

drinker · #day-beer · #no-alcohol-gathering · #mocktail have become everyday hashtags.
