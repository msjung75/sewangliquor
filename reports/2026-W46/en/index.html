HOW TRENDING SHOPS SELL DRINKS

They don't sell drinks separately —
they sell them bundled

① Highballs as the signatureYakitori Janjan
Seoul·Gyeonggi highball house
A skewer izakaya on both of GQ's 'top highball houses' lists, Seoul and Gyeonggi. With branches across Seoul and Gyeonggi including Sharosu-gil, Sadang, Seolleung, Suwon and Beomgye, it sells highballs together with skewer sets (Seolleung: 5 kinds for KRW 20,000).
Same approach — Yukhoegwan Pocha in Yeouido writes the whisky name on the menu, as in 'Maker's Mark highball KRW 11,000' (10/29 review). One line with a name (e.g. Kakubin highball) explains the price better than 'just a highball'.
② The middle bundles drinks tooSikdang Minhong · Hakdong, Gangnam
10 courses + unlimited KRW 98,000
Includes nomihodai (all-you-can-drink draft beer, highballs and fresh-fruit sours) (11/4 review). A Japanese course sells a scaled-down version of the 'drinks included' deal only hotels used to offer.
③ One bottle of wine per 4-topYear-end at BBQ places
750㎖ = about 5 glasses
As small 'own team only' year-end parties grow, BBQ places are pitching Hanwoo + wine pairings. One glass each for 4 people — one bottle fits one table. For a table of 2, 2 highballs are the same share.
④ The queue-worthy street bar: 'one cold glass'Dalmaji Gwangjang BBQ
Give the set a name
It names its menu bundles, like the 'Ready to Hang Out set KRW 53,000', and pushes ice-cold draft beer (KRW 6,000 in reviews) as its signature glass.
Drink prices in ② and ④ come from customer reviews and may differ from official prices. Shop addresses checked on KakaoMap.

Seoul pork belly dining-out price, 200g average KRW 21,321 × 1.5 + kimchi-jjigae set KRW 9,092 (nationwide) = KRW 41,074. Swap in your own prices.
Dining-out prices rose +2.9% in October (the highest since February this year). So 'unlimited' doesn't fit this price range — bundle by the glass or bottle. Cost of 1 highball = 40㎖ whisky about KRW 1,500 + soda, ice and lemon about KRW 400. Wine at a supply price of KRW 7,000–8,000 still leaves about KRW 24,000 per bottle when sold at KRW 32,000.
