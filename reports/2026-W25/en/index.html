One-line definition — a place you go to "listen, " not just to "drink"

A listening bar is a venue where music is the main act, played through vintage speakers and LPs on

a turntable. The opposite of a loud club — good sound, low lighting and a single drink make it an

"immersive music space. " 100 yrsHistory of the music café 1920s Tokyo → today's listening bar
