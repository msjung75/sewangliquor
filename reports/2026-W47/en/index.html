ANNOUNCED · 10 NOV

Japan's top 200 yakitori shops: 25 new this year

25shops

'Hyakumeiten' is the list in which Tabelog, Japan's largest restaurant rating site, picks the top 100 shops by score in each genre. Yakitori gets 100 each for East and West Japan; this is the 8th edition. Of these, 25 are listed for the first time (East 12 · West 13).

Bars show the number of shops by prefecture among the 200 (top 5). Ranked by Tabelog score as of 10 Sep. 'First time' means a shop never listed in any Hyakumeiten genre before; shops that moved or reopened are included.
WHY LOOKBecause the list is ranked by score, it shows shops rated highly right now. 15 of the 25 new entries opened in 2024 or later.

03
NEW ENTRIES ② WHY DINERS CHOSE THEM

What diners wrote in reviews:
first plate · timing · one glass

We read Tabelog reviews (Jun–Nov 2026) for the 5 most-reviewed shops and sorted what diners repeatedly praised into food · operations · drinks. Drawbacks are noted too.

Yakitori Taniguchi Tenjinbashi 焼鳥谷口 天神橋Osaka · score 4.24 · 145 reviews
Food
It opens, from the first plate, with near-raw chicken such as kombu-cured tenderloin. The most common comment: Kyotamba Kosaka chicken grilled crisp outside, juicy inside.
Ops
10-seat counter, two seatings at 17:30 · 20:30. No entry if more than 15 minutes late — in return, every guest gets the course at the same pace.
Drink
Reviews keep mentioning drinks recommended to match the food.
See photos on Tabelog
Sanzen 焼鳥 燦然Osaka · score 3.92 · 140 reviews
Food
Plates that show off freshness, like white liver sashimi · breast sashimi, come early in the course.
Ops
Titanium skewers, which conduct heat well, and bamboo skewers are used by ingredient; chicken is brushed with sesame and rice-bran oil, vegetables with chicken fat — diners copied the shop's explanation straight into their reviews. 8 seats, two seatings.
Drink
Even review titles say 'Kosaka chicken × wine pairing'. The pairing is sold as a named course from the start — 7 wines approx. KRW 67,000 (¥8,000).
Cons
Some rate value for money as only average.
See photos on Tabelog
Yamanaka 焼鳥 山中Hiroshima · score 3.73 · 27 reviews
Food
Aperitif → breast · thigh tataki → tsukune. Praise that the white liver is in a different league from other chicken.
Ops
One course, everyone starts together at 18:00 or 19:00. 'This much food at this price' — generous portions for the price come up again and again.
Drink
A glass of aperitif is included in the course, plus a 3-drink set approx. KRW 25,000 (¥3,000) and even a non-alcoholic pairing tea.
Cons
Occasional complaints that smoke fills the room.
See photos on Tabelog
Torifumi 焼鳥 鳥ふみSaga · score 3.64 · 80 reviews
Food
Aged Kuro-Satsuma chicken breast and thigh, plus rare cuts served only that day (back liver, diaphragm, gizzard) — the reason to come back changes every time.
Ops
13-seat hinoki counter. Many reviews say you won't get a seat without booking.
Drink
A regular: 'Here I always go for a Laphroaig highball' — a highball is approx. KRW 7,400 (¥880). The reason: its smoky aroma suits the skewers. Local sake comes in two sizes, glass or gō.
Cons
Occasional comments that drinks come out slowly.
See photos on Tabelog
Torikura とりくら TenmonkanKagoshima · score 3.64 · 71 reviews
Food
Kuro-Satsuma chicken skewers and vegetable rolls. Most reviews praise the grilling and the juiciness.
Ops
Starts with a short course, bookable on the day, approx. KRW 21,000–29,000 (¥2,500–3,500), then carries on à la carte. Easy as a second stop.
Drink
Praise that 'it's a casual skewer shop, yet they use separate red and white wine glasses'. Satsuma shochu and nomihodai (all-you-can-drink) too.
Cons
Some complaints about sold-out items, slow service and the cover snack.
See photos on Tabelog
Reviews are individual diners' opinions. We picked only points that overlapped across several reviews, and say so where a review repeats the shop's own explanation. All 12 in East Japan mark wine as 'available', but a priced pairing exists at only 2 of all 25 (both in West Japan).
① One beer company, named on the menu8 of 11
Master's Dream · The Premium Malt's · Sapporo Lager(a.k.a. Akaboshi)
Of the 11 shops where the beer brand could be confirmed, 8 carry beer from only one company (2 confirmed only through Suntory's '神泡 (Kamiawa) certified' sign — shops whose pouring Suntory has certified).
MOZU also names Suntory The Premium Malt's on its beer menu — so guests can order by name.
② Highballs named by whiskyAll 5 that list highball prices
Base approx. KRW 5,100–7,400 (¥605–880) + a premium tier
Named after the whisky, e.g. 'Kaku (角) highball approx. KRW 6,400 (¥770)'. 4 add a premium tier above it, such as Hakushu or Yamazaki, at approx. KRW 7,400–23,000 (¥880–2,700).
If your shop also lists the whisky name and price, guests can easily see the difference between the base highball and the premium tier.
③ Drinks as a 'set'Sanzen · Yamanaka
Pairing approx. KRW 25,000–67,000 (¥3,000–8,000)
Sanzen 燦然 (Osaka): 7 wines approx. KRW 67,000 (¥8,000) · 4 wines approx. KRW 42,000 (¥5,000), with a sommelier. Yamanaka 焼鳥 山中 (Hiroshima): 3-drink set approx. KRW 25,000 (¥3,000), plus a non-alcoholic pairing tea.
In Seoul, MOZU has 3-glass wine and 5-glass sake sets, and Las Vegas has a 3-sake sampler. Korean shops also bundle several glasses for guests to pick.
④ Sake in two sizesTorifumi · Saga
From approx. KRW 4,200 (¥500) a glass
4 local sakes sold in two sizes, 'glass / gō (180 ml)'. Glass and gō prices approx. KRW 4,200–11,000 (¥500–1,260). Start small; go bigger if you like it.
Based on the 10 shops that posted drink menus on Tabelog, plus shop intros. The other 15 have no drink menu, so they were counted as 'not confirmed', not 'none'.

08
FOR YOUR SHOP · TO-DO

One line of times, one line of drink names,
one 3-glass set

✓
① Add 'Seating 1 18:00 · Seating 2 20:00' to December booking info
Start with the Fridays when year-end groups crowd in (4, 11, 18 Dec). Add a one-line lateness/cancellation rule too — 13 of Japan's new entries set start times, and 9 write lateness/cancellation rules
✓
② Put names in the drinks section of your menu
'Draft beer' → the brand name; 'Highball' → the whisky name, like 'Kaku highball'. One base price + one premium tier is enough
✓
③ Add a '3-glass set' option to skewer sets
Work out the multi-glass bundle used by Yamanaka in Japan and MOZU and Las Vegas in Korea at your own prices — see the (example) below

Cost of 3 glasses approx. 6,000 (highball approx. 2,000 × 2 + 1 glass of wine approx. 2,000)= approx. KRW 12,000 left from the set's drinks
Highball cost per glass = 40 ml whisky approx. KRW 1,500 + soda, ice, lemon approx. KRW 400. Wine per glass = a KRW 10,000 wholesale bottle ÷ 5 glasses. Glass of wine KRW 9,000 and set KRW 18,000 are examples — plug in your own prices.
5 skewers + 3-glass set = approx. KRW 36,000 per person. The set pays off when guests who used to stop at 1–2 glasses drink 3 — for guests who already ordered 3 separately, profit per glass falls. The margin is based on VAT-inclusive prices (approx. KRW 10,000 excluding VAT). Unlike all-you-can-drink, the glass count is fixed, so the cost is visible in advance.
