01BBQ · grill houses

Lemon highball

Whisky + soda water

FoodGrilled meat · chicken
TasteLemon aroma · crisp fizz
By the glass · mixed in-house
One line for your menu

“A lemony, fizzy glass to go with your grill.”

Carried by SewangMake the same 1:4 highball with on-trade Suntory Kakubin. For sizes and terms, ask your Sewang sales rep.

Maker’s recipeWhisky : soda 1:4 [3]

Measure it out
Base recipe

30 mL whisky + 120 mL soda + lemon + ice. Use a jigger for the same taste every time.

Getting ready

Check ice, glasses and cold soda first. Set the price from real ingredient cost and pour size.

02Korean · jeon houses

Clean cheongju

Served chilled

FoodJeon · suyuk · clear soups
TasteClear, clean taste
Small bottles
One line for your menu

“Clean, chilled cheongju for jeon and suyuk.”

Seoul case · Bangi-dong, SongpaSaru Oden Bangi serves warm jeongjong (Korean-style sake) with oden. The shop’s own post: “After work, shoulder to shoulder, a glass of warm jeongjong.” In December, see ‘Warm sake in December’ below.

Maker’s product infoCheongju served chilled [4]

Start with small bottles, chilled
How to choose

Start with a clean product meant to be served cold. Put the ABV and bottle size from the actual label on your menu.

How to serve

Start with one small bottle and small cups. Plan around fridge space and party size.

Warm sake in December

On cold days, some guests ask for warm jeongjong. For products that suit warming, ask your Sewang sales rep.

Sauvignon Blanc

Dry white wine

FoodSashimi · seafood · smoked salmon
TasteFresh acidity · fruity
Chilled · start by the bottle
One line for your menu

“A fresh white wine for your seafood.”

Seoul cases · this winter’s yellowtail seasonSukseonghoe Hwado (Mapo) opens big-yellowtail bookings at midnight, 7 days ahead. Kuhichi Apgujeong (Gangnam) offers a limited yellowtail + raw shrimp plate. Time to add a drink beside seasonal sashimi.

Importer’s food pairingSeafood · smoked salmon [5]

Start with bottle sales
How to choose

Pick a style with acidity that suits seafood. Check taste differences by tasting or from the importer’s notes.

How to serve

Serve chilled and start with bottle sales. Add by-the-glass once you see orders and how you can store opened bottles.

04Pizza · pasta

Brut sparkling

Dry sparkling wine

FoodPizza · oil-based pasta
TasteNot sweet · bubbles
Chilled · start by the bottle
One line for your menu

“A dry sparkling wine to enjoy with pizza.”

Importer’s food pairingPizza · oil-based pasta [6]

Serve cold, keep the bubbles
How to choose

Pick a dry Brut-style wine. The product we referenced is ‘Brut Nature’, the driest sweetness category of sparkling wine (drier than Brut). Check how sweet guests like it and the actual product’s profile.

How to serve

Start with bottle sales. To sell by the glass, get sparkling stoppers and plan storage after opening.
