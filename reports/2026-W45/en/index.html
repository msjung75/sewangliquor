ONE-PAGE BOOKING NOTICE · TO-DO

The three lines that go before the menu

3lines

Deposit · refund deadline · end time. Copy it and change only the amounts and times.

COPY & USE
[December group bookings open]
· Groups of 6+: deposit KRW 20,000 per person — deducted from the bill on arrival
· Cancellation: full refund up to 2 days before · 50% 1 day before · no refund on the day or for a no-show without notice
(On a no-show the deposit is not refunded and is kept as the cancellation fee — within the Consumer Dispute Resolution Standards cap of 40% of the total bill · advisory standard)
· Bookings may be cancelled automatically 20 minutes after the reserved time
· Starts 6pm · ends 9pm course KRW ○○ (one main: Hanwoo sirloin or abalone) · meal-only also available
· Book: Naver Booking / phone 02-○○○ (confirmation text sent once the deposit is received)
The Korean original can be copied from the KO page (first button in the language switcher at the top). KRW 20,000 is the survey median; the refund ladder follows 2 surveyed shops. Keep the deposit under 40% of the total bill (at KRW 50,000 per head, KRW 20,000 per person is the cap). Not legal advice.
